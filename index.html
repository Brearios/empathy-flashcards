Empathy Flashcards

How to practice

  1. Read the situation.
  2. Say your own response out loud before tapping, even if it feels awkward. That's where the practice happens.
  3. Tap to see three good options and one trap to avoid. They're examples, not the only right answers.
  4. Tap again for the next card.

The moves

Name the feeling
Put a word to what they might be feeling.
Validate
Let them know their reaction makes sense.
Reflect back
Say what you heard, in your own words.
Get curious
Ask an open question and let them lead.
Be present
Show you're staying. Nothing to fix.
Offer help
Offer something concrete they can turn down.
Celebrate
Match their joy and keep it about them.
Own it
Take responsibility, no excuses.
Thank them
Appreciate them for telling you, or for what they did.

Common traps

Silver lining
"At least…" skips past how they feel.
Fixing / Advice
Solving before they feel heard.
Minimizing
Making it smaller than it is to them.
One-upping
Turning it into a story about you.
Getting defensive
Protecting yourself instead of listening.
Yes, but
Agreeing, then taking it back.
